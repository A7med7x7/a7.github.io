## About Me

I'm Ahmed. I most most of my life learning to function, learning how to learng, being amazed and exploring and casually thinking. I write from somewhere between introspection and confusion, which is probably why most of what ends up here doesn't fit neatly into a category.
This blog is where I think out loud. mostly about human experience of grief, about what I'm learning and why most of what I was told about life is wrong. about wrong lessong that I learned. about myself and about the world.
The posts here are personal essays, occasional letters, learning notes, and fragments that didn't know where else to go. If you're looking for polished opinions and clean conclusions, this is probably not the right place. but if you've ever felt something precise that you didn't have a name for yet, maybe something here will name it for you.


## About This Blog
