Change is not only about will power. you can [fight your inner conflicts](https://a7med7x7.github.io/posts/choosing-not-to-drift/). you can [decide to improve](https://a7med7x7.github.io/posts/sick-of-yourself/). you can push yourself daily. but there is another factor that matters just as much: timing. some forms of change does not happen just because you want it to. it happens when life gives it space to grow. it happens when conditions are in favorable.

You cannot always heal in the same environment that hurt you. sometimes the ground itself keeps reopening the wound. and sometimes you cannot confront something if it is no longer in front of you. healing needs the right conditions. it needs a season where [your surroundings](https://a7med7x7.github.io/posts/not-broken-just-suboptimal/) do not quietly undo the progress you are trying to make. because if the change cannot survive your environment, if does not surface on it's own and being pulled, it is not stable yet.

Healing alone is hard. healing around people is also hard. but healing becomes possible when your internal intention and your external life stop fighting each other. when what you want inside matches where you stand outside, things begin to move.

Alignment does not come from waiting. you still have to act. you still have to step into situations that scare you. sometimes growth requires you to stay in moments that awaken what you buried long ago.

 You should not rely on the idea that life will always give you the same opportunity again. moments do not repeat themselves perfectly. when the conditions are right, act. when they are not, prepare yourself so you will be ready when they are.

Do not stay frozen by fear when the wind is finally in your favor, when life opens a window, move, because sometimes change requires two things: readiness and clarity inside, and opportunity outside. when both meet, that is your season. and if you step forward with intention instead of hesitation, you will eventually reach a different shore.
