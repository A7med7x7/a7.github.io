There is something that can make everything harder. take a problem that is manageable, something you can think through, something you can slowly untangle. now add one variable to it. add doing it alone. suddenly the same situation feels heavier. the same thoughts feel darker. the same obstacle feels impossible. changing alone, healing alone, fighting yourself alone, these do not simply double the weight. they multiply it.
<!--more-->
Whenever I try to overcome something that has always blocked me, I feel that weight clearly. it is not only the problem itself. it is the loneliness around it. the weight of doing it alone. the silence that amplifies every doubt. there is something exhausting about trying to outgrow yourself in isolation.

One pattern I recognized early in my life is that living in my head is directly proportional to my isolation. they work in both directions. when I am not socially active, I drown in my thoughts. when I drown in my thoughts, I withdraw socially. it becomes a loop. I would sit in my room trying to make sense of something I could not even properly name. the thoughts would grow larger, heavier, more dramatic than they probably were.

And then something simple would happen. a walk with Abdalwahab. nothing revolutionary. just being outside, laughing, talking about random things. somehow that was enough to pull me out of my own shadow. the joy of whatever we did together would linger even after I returned to my room. I would sit there again, but this time the same thoughts looked different. they felt smaller. less absolute. less catastrophic.

What changed was not necessarily the problem. what changed was the despair attached to it. I began to see that when I am alone for too long, I tend to multiply how terrible everything feels. I blur the line between the issue itself and the emotional weight surrounding it. sometimes staying alone long enough even unlocks fears that were not there in the first place. but this is also to admit the power of relationships, that they work like magic, it takes only couple of minutes, to move from despair, to happiness, and appreciating things that you would have never thought about minutes ago. as if life is just simple around people, and when you were on your own, you were overcomplicating it.

This is not a call to dismiss your thoughts by constantly socializing. I have already written about not [erasing the pain](https://a7med7x7.github.io/posts/on-comparing-suffering-without-erasing-it/) in different situations, [sitting with uncomfortable thoughts](https://a7med7x7.github.io/posts/the-condition-of-seeing/) can lead to necessary change. reflection is not the enemy. but isolation without awareness can distort perspective. and amplify the despair, you have to learn the difference between processing and spiraling.

Healing alone is possible. but it is heavier. and sometimes the bravest thing you can do is allow someone to walk beside you, not to solve your mind, but to soften its sharpest edges. clarity does not always come from thinking harder. sometimes it comes from stepping outside your own echo.
